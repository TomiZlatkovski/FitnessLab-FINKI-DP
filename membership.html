GOOD TO KNOW

Membership questions.

Can I visit before joining?

Contact the team to ask about visiting and any current introductory options.

Do I need training experience to join?

No. Talk to a coach about your experience and goals. Sessions should be scaled to your current ability and comfort.

What are the membership terms?

Ask the team for current pricing, billing, cancellation, and pause terms before joining.

Are fitness assessments included?

Testing is not necessary for everyone. Ask what a test measures, who conducts it, what it costs, and how the result would change your training plan.
